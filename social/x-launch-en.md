I built an English-first, Chinese-supported daily intelligence briefing system for AI, markets, and medtech.

It turns 10 high-signal stories into a mobile-friendly magazine PDF—with a strict 7-day anti-repetition ledger, contextual terminology cards, bilingual analysis, next triggers, and page-by-page layout QA.

I built it because a useful daily brief should advance your understanding, not recycle yesterday's headlines.
